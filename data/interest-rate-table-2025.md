> **Superseded.** This rate schedule applied from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 and has been
> replaced by `interest-rate-table-2026.md`, effective 2026-01-01. It is kept here for
> reference and for questions about what a customer who opened a deposit in 2025 was quoted.

Annual interest rates for standard term deposits, 2025 schedule:

| Term      | RON   | EUR   | USD   |
|-----------|-------|-------|-------|
| 1 month   | 3.10% | 1.20% | 1.40% |
| 3 months  | 3.60% | 1.50% | 1.70% |
| 6 months  | 4.00% | 1.80% | 2.00% |
| 12 months | 4.40% | 2.10% | 2.30% |
| 18 months | 4.30% | 2.00% | 2.20% |
| 24 months | 4.20% | 1.90% | 2.10% |

Rates are gross annual rates, before withholding tax (see `tax-on-interest.md`). Interest is
paid at maturity for terms up to 12 months, and annually for terms above 12 months.

These rates do not include the Young Savers bonus or the Loyalty Rate bonus described in
`loyalty-rate-eligibility.md`.
