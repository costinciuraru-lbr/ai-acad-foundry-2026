Breaking a term deposit before its maturity date is allowed at any time, subject to the
following:

- **Interest forfeiture.** All interest accrued since opening (or since the last renewal) is
  forfeited. The customer receives back only the principal, minus the administrative fee
  below. This applies regardless of currency or term.
- **Administrative fee.** A flat fee of **25 RON** (or the equivalent of 5 EUR / 5 USD for
  foreign-currency deposits) is deducted from the principal at the time of early withdrawal.
- **Partial withdrawal.** Partial early withdrawal is not offered on term deposits — breaking
  a deposit closes it in full. Customers who need part of the funds must close the whole
  deposit.

Notice savings accounts are different: they are never "broken," but withdrawals require the
notice period described in `notice-period-policy-v2.md`, and requesting funds before that
notice period elapses is not possible through any channel.

Exceptions: no fee or interest forfeiture applies if the withdrawal is due to the death of the
account holder (verified by the estate) or a court order. These exceptions require a branch
visit and cannot be processed through the mobile app or call centre.

For the step-by-step process of breaking a term deposit, see `closing-early-procedure.md`.
