## Fees

All fees for term deposits and notice savings accounts, in RON (or currency equivalent):

| Fee                                   | Amount           | Notes                              |
|----------------------------------------|------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Account opening                        | Free             | Any channel                         |
| Monthly statement (paper, by post)     | 8 RON            | E-statements are free               |
| Monthly statement (electronic)         | Free             | Delivered in the mobile app         |
| Early withdrawal administrative fee    | 25 RON           | See `early-withdrawal-policy.md`    |
| Balance certificate (branch)           | 15 RON           | For visa, court or notary use       |
| Balance certificate (mobile app)       | Free             | Self-service, instant               |
| Deposit closure at maturity            | Free             | Any channel                         |
| Duplicate contract copy                | 10 RON           | Branch only                         |
| International wire on maturity payout  | 35 RON           | Only for payouts to non-Libra IBANs |

## Vulnerable customers

Fees are debited from the customer's linked current account, or from the deposit principal if
no current account is linked (e.g., some Young Savers deposits). Fees do not apply to
customers in the Compliance-flagged "vulnerable customer" category, per internal policy —
branch staff should check this flag before charging any fee on this list.

## Out of scope

This schedule does not include lending fees, card fees, or investment product fees, which are
documented separately and outside this corpus.
