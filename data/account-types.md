Libra Bank offers term deposits and notice savings accounts in three currencies: RON, EUR and
USD. This document lists the products a retail customer can open and the basic eligibility
rule for each.

## Standard term deposit

Available in RON, EUR or USD. Minimum opening amount is 1,000 RON or the equivalent in
EUR/USD. Terms available: 1, 3, 6, 12, 18 and 24 months. Open to any customer aged 18 or over
with an active current account at Libra Bank.

## Young Savers term deposit

RON only, for customers aged 14 to 25. Minimum opening amount is 200 RON. Pays the same base
rate as the standard 12-month term deposit plus a fixed 0.20 percentage point bonus. Requires
a parent or legal guardian as co-holder for customers under 18.

## Notice savings account

RON, EUR or USD. No fixed term; funds are available subject to the notice period described in
`notice-period-policy-v2.md`. Minimum balance to earn interest is 500 RON or equivalent.

## Instant-access savings account

RON, EUR or USD. No minimum balance, no notice period, lowest rate of the three products. Not
covered further in this corpus.

## Out of scope

Corporate and business deposit accounts are a separate product line, documented outside this
corpus, and are not covered by the retail rates or procedures described here.
