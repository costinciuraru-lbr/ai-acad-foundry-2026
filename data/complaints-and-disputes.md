This document describes how a customer raises a complaint about a term deposit or savings
account, and what response times to expect. It does not cover general banking complaints
(cards, loans) outside the deposit products in this corpus.

## Channels

Complaints can be raised through the mobile app (Help → Complaint), by phone through Card and
Deposit Services, or in writing at any branch. All channels are treated equally — there is no
channel that gets faster handling than another.

## Acknowledgement

Every complaint receives a written acknowledgement (email or in-app message) within 2 business
days, including a reference number.

## Resolution target

Libra Bank aims to resolve deposit-related complaints within 15 calendar days of
acknowledgement. Complex cases (e.g., involving a joint account dispute per
`joint-account-rules.md`, or a disputed early-withdrawal fee) may take up to 30 calendar days,
and the customer is informed if this longer timeline applies.

## Common complaint categories

This corpus is relevant to: disagreement over an early withdrawal fee
(`early-withdrawal-policy.md`), a missed Loyalty Rate bonus (`loyalty-rate-eligibility.md`), a
maturity payout sent to the wrong account (`auto-renewal-policy.md`), or a notice-period
withdrawal that took longer than expected (`notice-period-policy-v2.md`).

## Escalation

If unresolved after the resolution target, the customer can escalate to the Libra Bank
Ombudsman office, and after that to the national Banking Alternative Dispute Resolution
scheme. Details are provided in the acknowledgement message.
