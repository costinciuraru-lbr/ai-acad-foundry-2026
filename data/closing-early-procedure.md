title: Breaking a Term Deposit Early — Step by Step

audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01

---

Use this procedure when a customer wants to withdraw funds from a term deposit before its
maturity date. Read `early-withdrawal-policy.md` first — this document only covers the
mechanical steps, not the fee and interest rules.

1. **Locate the deposit.** In the mobile app: Savings → the specific deposit → "Close early."
   In branch: the officer opens the deposit contract in the core banking system.
2. **Show the payout preview.** The system calculates and displays the exact payout: principal
   minus the 25 RON (or currency equivalent) administrative fee, with zero interest. The
   customer must see this figure before confirming — silently returning less than the
   preview is not permitted.
3. **Confirm identity.** Mobile app: PIN or biometric. Branch: government ID plus signature
   match against the account specimen.
4. **Confirm the closure.** The customer explicitly confirms they understand the interest
   forfeiture. In the app this is a checkbox with the forfeiture amount shown in RON; in
   branch it is a signed acknowledgement form.
5. **Process the payout.** Funds land in the linked current account within the same business
   day for app and branch requests submitted before 16:00. Requests after 16:00 are processed
   the next business day.
6. **Close the contract.** The deposit contract is marked closed and archived. A closure
   confirmation is sent by email or shown in the app; branch customers can request a paper
   copy for 10 RON per `fee-schedule.md`.

If the customer is asking about a notice savings account rather than a term deposit, this
procedure does not apply — see `notice-period-policy-v2.md` instead, since a notice account is
never "broken," only withdrawn from after the notice period.
