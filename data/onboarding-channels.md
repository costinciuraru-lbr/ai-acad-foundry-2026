Term deposits and notice savings accounts can be opened through three channels. This document
compares them; the step-by-step mechanics for the mobile app and branch are the same flow
described in `opening-procedure.md` — this page only covers channel-specific differences.

## Mobile app

Available to any existing customer with online banking already activated. No new identity
verification is needed — the customer is already known. Fastest channel, funds must come from
a Libra Bank current account already linked to the app.

## Online banking (web)

Same eligibility and speed as the mobile app, for customers who prefer a desktop browser.
Functionally identical to the app for deposit products.

## Branch

Required for: customers without online banking activated, funding from a third-party account
(see step 4 of `opening-procedure.md`), joint accounts opened by holders who cannot each
confirm digitally (see `joint-account-rules.md`), and any customer who is not yet a Libra Bank
customer at all — new customers cannot open a deposit before completing identity verification
(KYC) in branch, which requires a valid government ID and proof of address.

## What no channel supports

Opening a deposit by phone alone is not offered — Card and Deposit Services can answer
questions and direct customers to the app or a branch, but cannot open a deposit account over
a phone call, for identity verification reasons.

New-to-bank customers should be directed to a branch first; existing customers should
generally be directed to the app unless one of the branch-only conditions above applies.
