title: Withholding Tax on Deposit Interest

Interest earned on term deposits and notice savings accounts held by individual (non-business)
customers is subject to withholding tax, deducted automatically by Libra Bank before the
interest is paid or added to the deposit.

**Rate.** The withholding tax rate is **10%** of gross interest earned, applied at the moment
interest is paid — at maturity for terms up to 12 months, or annually for longer terms (see
`interest-rate-table-2026.md` for payment timing per term).

**Example.** A 12-month RON deposit earning 5.00% gross on a 10,000 RON principal accrues 500
RON gross interest at maturity; 50 RON (10%) is withheld, and the customer receives 450 RON
net interest alongside the returned principal.

**Reporting.** Libra Bank issues an annual interest statement showing gross interest, tax
withheld, and net interest for each customer, available in the mobile app under
Documents → Tax Statements. Customers do not need to declare this interest separately unless
required by their personal tax situation — this document does not constitute tax advice.

**Business accounts.** Withholding tax treatment for legal entities differs and is not covered
by this document.

This rate applies uniformly across RON, EUR and USD deposits, and across all products
described in `account-types.md`.
