---
title: Automatic Renewal (Roll-Over) at Maturity
product: deposits
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01
version: 1
---

Customers choose their maturity instruction when opening a term deposit (see step 6 of
`opening-procedure.md`). This document describes what happens under each instruction.

**Auto-renew (default for most customers).** At maturity, the principal plus any interest
earned is placed into a new deposit of the same term, at whatever the current base rate is on
the renewal date — not the rate the original deposit was opened at. Any Loyalty Rate bonus is
re-evaluated at renewal, using the eligibility rules in `loyalty-rate-eligibility.md` as they
stand on the renewal date, not the original opening date. A customer who lost eligibility
(e.g., closed their other Libra Bank product) will renew at the base rate without the bonus.

**Pay out to linked current account.** The full amount (principal plus interest) is
transferred automatically to the customer's linked current account on the maturity date, and
the deposit is closed. No further action is required from the customer.

**Pay out to a different IBAN.** Available only when set up in branch. Subject to the wire
transfer fee in `fee-schedule.md` if the destination IBAN is not a Libra Bank account.

Customers can change their maturity instruction at any time before maturity, through the app
or in branch, with no fee. A change made on the maturity date itself may not take effect in
time — this must be done at least one business day before maturity.

If a customer takes no action and no instruction was set, the system defaults to auto-renew.
