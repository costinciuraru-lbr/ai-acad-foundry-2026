> **Current policy**, effective 2026-01-15. Replaces `notice-period-policy-v1.md`. The notice
> period was extended from 30 to 45 days as part of the 2026 liquidity policy update.

Customers holding a notice savings account must give advance notice before withdrawing funds.
Under this (current) policy, the required notice period is **45 calendar days** from the date
the withdrawal request is submitted, regardless of the amount requested.

A withdrawal request is submitted through the mobile app (Savings → notice account → "Request
withdrawal") or in branch. The 45-day countdown starts on the date the request is confirmed,
not the date it is initiated — an incomplete request (e.g., missing identity verification)
does not start the countdown.

Once the notice period elapses, funds become available for transfer to the linked current
account for 5 business days. If not withdrawn within that window, the withdrawal request
expires and the customer must submit a new one, restarting the notice period.

There is no way to expedite or waive the notice period under this policy, including for
emergencies — this is unlike term deposits, which can always be broken early subject to
`early-withdrawal-policy.md`.

Requests submitted before 2026-01-15 under the old 30-day notice period were honoured at 30
days; only requests submitted on or after that date are subject to the new 45-day period.
