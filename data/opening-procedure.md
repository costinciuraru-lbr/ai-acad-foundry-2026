This is the full procedure for opening a standard term deposit, valid for the mobile app,
online banking and branch channels unless a step says otherwise.

1. **Confirm eligibility.** The customer must be 18 or over (or use the Young Savers product,
   see `account-types.md`) and hold an active Libra Bank current account in the currency they
   want to deposit in, or be willing to open one first.
2. **Choose the product.** Select currency (RON, EUR or USD) and term (1, 3, 6, 12, 18 or 24
   months). The interest rate shown at this step is always the current base rate from
   `interest-rate-table-2026.md` — bonuses are applied in step 5.
3. **Enter the amount.** Minimum 1,000 RON or equivalent for the standard product, 200 RON for
   Young Savers. The app will reject amounts below the minimum with an explanation.
4. **Choose the funding source.** Funds are debited immediately from the linked current
   account. Funding from a third-party account is not supported in the app; it requires a
   branch visit with a wire transfer.
5. **Loyalty check.** The system (or branch staff, per `loyalty-rate-eligibility.md`) checks
   whether the customer qualifies for the Loyalty Rate bonus, and if so applies it before
   showing the final confirmed rate.
6. **Choose the maturity instruction.** The customer selects what happens at maturity:
   auto-renew (see `auto-renewal-policy.md`), pay out to the linked current account, or pay
   out to a different IBAN (branch only, subject to the wire fee in `fee-schedule.md`).
7. **Review and confirm.** The app or branch officer shows the final rate, maturity date, and
   maturity instruction for confirmation. In the app, confirmation requires the mobile PIN or
   biometric authentication.
8. **Receive the contract.** A digital contract is issued immediately in the app, or a paper
   contract in branch (duplicate copies cost 10 RON per `fee-schedule.md`).

The whole process takes under five minutes in the app for an existing customer, or about
fifteen minutes in branch for a first-time depositor.
