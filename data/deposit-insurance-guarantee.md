All retail deposits at Libra Bank — including term deposits, notice savings accounts, and
instant-access savings accounts — are covered by the national Bank Deposit Guarantee Fund, up
to the legal ceiling.

**Coverage limit.** Deposits are guaranteed up to **100,000 EUR equivalent per depositor, per
bank**, regardless of currency, number of accounts, or number of products held. If a customer
holds both a term deposit and a notice savings account, the two balances are added together
for the purpose of the limit — the guarantee is not per product.

**Joint accounts.** For joint accounts (see `joint-account-rules.md`), each account holder is
covered up to 100,000 EUR equivalent individually, calculated on their share of the joint
balance combined with their other deposits at Libra Bank.

**What is covered.** Principal plus interest accrued up to the date of any bank default event,
converted to EUR at the exchange rate on that date for RON and USD balances.

**What is not covered.** Investment products, structured deposits, and any product not
explicitly listed above are outside the guarantee scheme — but Libra Bank does not offer those
products to retail customers in the first place (see `00-overview.md`).

This is a summary for customer-facing use. The authoritative legal text is the Bank Deposit
Guarantee Fund's published regulation, available on request from any branch.
