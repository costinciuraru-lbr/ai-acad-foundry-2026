## Overview

Libra Bank issues two debit card tiers to retail customers: **Libra Basic** and **Libra
Premium**. Both attach to the customer's current account and can be requested at account
opening or added later at no charge, in the branch or the mobile app.

## Visual identification

- **Libra Basic** — plain white / very light grey card body, Mastercard logo in the
  top-right corner, no chip artwork or gold/metallic elements visible, no other branding.
- **Libra Premium** — issued in two color variants, either is a valid match: a matte black
  card with gold-toned chip, text and "gold debit" wording, Mastercard logo top-right; or a
  solid pink card, Mastercard logo bottom-right. Either variant is clearly more decorated
  (gold accents, or a bold solid color) than the plain white Basic card.

## Libra Basic

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Annual fee | Free |
| Cashback | 0.5% on all purchases, capped at 50 RON/month |
| Contactless limit (no PIN) | 100 RON per transaction |
| ATM withdrawals at Libra ATMs | Free, unlimited |
| ATM withdrawals at other banks' ATMs | 6 RON per withdrawal |
| Travel insurance | Not included |
| Airport lounge access | Not included |
| Eligibility | Any retail current account holder |

## Libra Premium

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Annual fee | 150 RON/year, waived if the average monthly account balance is 20,000 RON or more |
| Cashback | 1.5% on all purchases, capped at 250 RON/month |
| Contactless limit (no PIN) | 500 RON per transaction |
| ATM withdrawals at Libra ATMs | Free, unlimited |
| ATM withdrawals at other banks' ATMs, worldwide | Free, up to 4 withdrawals/month, 6 RON per withdrawal after that |
| Travel insurance | Included — medical + baggage, EU and worldwide |
| Airport lounge access | 2 free visits/year, participating lounges |
| Eligibility | Average monthly balance of 5,000 RON or more over the prior 3 months, or an active salary deposit |

## Upgrading or downgrading

Customers can request a switch between Libra Basic and Libra Premium at any time, in the
branch or the mobile app. A new physical card is issued (free of charge); the old card is
deactivated once the new one is activated. There is no minimum period between switches.

## Out of scope

This document does not cover credit cards, business cards, or virtual/prepaid cards, which
are handled under separate product documentation not included in this corpus.
