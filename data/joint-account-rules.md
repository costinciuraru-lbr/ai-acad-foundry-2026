Term deposits and notice savings accounts can be opened jointly by up to four individual
holders. This document covers the rules specific to joint deposits; standard rules (rates,
fees, procedures) from the other documents in this corpus apply unless stated otherwise here.

## Opening

All joint holders must be present (in branch) or must each separately confirm through the app
(for digital joint accounts) at the time of opening. A joint deposit cannot be opened by one
holder acting alone, even with power of attorney, unless that power of attorney explicitly
covers deposit accounts.

## Operating mandate

At opening, holders choose either "any one signature" (any holder can instruct withdrawals or
closure alone) or "all signatures" (every holder must agree). This choice cannot be changed
after opening without closing and reopening the deposit.

## Early withdrawal

Under an "any one signature" mandate, any single holder can request early withdrawal per
`closing-early-procedure.md`, and the payout goes to the account's registered linked current
account regardless of which holder requested it. Under "all signatures," every holder must
confirm before the withdrawal is processed.

## Death of a holder

If one joint holder dies, the deposit does not automatically transfer to the surviving holders
— it is frozen pending an inheritance procedure, handled in branch with the estate's
documentation. This differs from the exception described in `early-withdrawal-policy.md`,
which covers the deposit of a sole holder.

## Deposit guarantee

See `deposit-insurance-guarantee.md` for how the coverage limit applies to joint accounts.
