> **Current schedule**, effective 2026-01-01. Replaces `interest-rate-table-2025.md`. Rates
> rose across all terms and currencies compared to 2025, reflecting the higher policy rate.

Annual interest rates for standard term deposits, 2026 schedule:

| Term      | RON   | EUR   | USD   |
|-----------|-------|-------|-------|
| 1 month   | 3.50% | 1.40% | 1.60% |
| 3 months  | 4.00% | 1.70% | 1.90% |
| 6 months  | 4.50% | 2.00% | 2.20% |
| 12 months | 5.00% | 2.30% | 2.50% |
| 18 months | 4.80% | 2.20% | 2.40% |
| 24 months | 4.60% | 2.10% | 2.30% |

Rates are gross annual rates, before withholding tax (see `tax-on-interest.md`). Interest is
paid at maturity for terms up to 12 months, and annually for terms above 12 months.

The Young Savers bonus (`account-types.md`) and the Loyalty Rate bonus
(`loyalty-rate-eligibility.md`) both stack on top of the base rate shown above, applied at the
time of opening and fixed for the life of the deposit. See `loyalty-rate-eligibility.md` for
who qualifies for the Loyalty Rate bonus and its size — the base rate above is only part of
what a loyalty customer actually earns.
