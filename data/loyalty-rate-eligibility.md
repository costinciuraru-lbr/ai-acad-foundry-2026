The Loyalty Rate is a bonus added on top of the standard term deposit rate (see
`interest-rate-table-2026.md` for base rates) for customers who meet all three conditions
below at the time of opening the deposit:

1. The customer has held an active Libra Bank current account for at least 12 consecutive
   months.
2. The customer has at least one other active product with Libra Bank — a credit card, a
   mortgage, or a standing salary deposit into a Libra Bank current account.
3. The term deposit being opened is for 6 months or longer.

Customers who meet all three conditions receive a bonus of **0.30 percentage points** added
to the base rate shown in the current interest rate table, for the full term of the deposit.
The bonus is fixed at opening and does not change if the customer later stops meeting
condition 1 or 2; it is re-evaluated only at renewal (see `auto-renewal-policy.md`).

The bonus does not apply to the Young Savers term deposit, which has its own fixed 0.20
percentage point bonus that cannot be combined with the Loyalty Rate.

Branch staff can check eligibility in the customer's profile under "Relationship tenure" and
"Active products." The bonus must be applied manually at account opening — it is not
calculated automatically by the core banking system as of this document's effective date.
